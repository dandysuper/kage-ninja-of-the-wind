A 2.5D open-world ninja RPG for the TIC-80 fantasy console. Climb anything, glide on a kite, survive the night and win back the Three Sacred Treasures from the Oni King.

Shuten-doji, the Oni King, has stolen the Magatama Jewel, the Yata Mirror and the sword Kusanagi. Without them the nights grow long and yokai roam the land. You are Kage, Sensei Hayate's student in the village of Hanamura. Finish your training, answer Lady Sayo's call at Kyo Castle, take the Treasures back from three great yokai, then cross the sealed bridge to Onigashima.

It plays like a pocket-sized mix of Breath of the Wild and Don't Starve: climb and glide anywhere you like, but never get caught in the dark.

## Features

- **A whole island to explore.** Eight regions seen through a rotatable isometric camera: Sakura Vale, Whispering Bamboo, Kyo Plains, snowy Mount Fuyu, the Haunted Wood, Minato Coast, Kappa Marsh and the volcanic island of Onigashima.
- **Climb anything.** Push into any cliff, wall or building to climb it. Glide on your kite, earn a double jump and swim across rivers, as long as your stamina wheel holds out.
- **Survive the night.** A full day lasts six minutes. After dark, step outside the light and the Yami comes for you, so carry a torch or lantern and build campfires. Keep yourself fed, and dress warm on Mount Fuyu.
- **Gather, craft and cook.** Chop trees and bamboo, mine rock and ore, and craft tools, light, armor and supplies across five tabs. Cook over a campfire, or combine ingredients in a pot for sushi, nabe stew, sakura dango, mochi and green tea.
- **Fight like a shinobi.** A three-hit katana combo, plunge attacks from the air and shuriken against bandits, ronin, wolves, boars, kappa, tengu, kitsune, oni, spiders, and the yurei and onibi that only come out at night.
- **Four boss battles.** Jorogumo the Spider Queen, Sojobo the Tengu Lord, Yamata no Orochi and Shuten-doji, the Oni King.
- **15 quests.** A six-part main story and nine side quests, from a lost cat to a bandit bounty, across five towns and a haunted temple.
- **Secrets to find.** Climb six pagodas to reveal the map and unlock fast travel. Clear eight spirit shrines for orbs and trade them for extra hearts or stamina. Find 19 hidden kodama, open 15 chests and clear 14 enemy camps.
- **An original soundtrack** in Japanese scales for koto, shakuhachi, shamisen, fue and taiko: seven music tracks that change with the region, the time of day and the battle, plus 40 sound effects.
- **Autosave.** The game saves at dawn, at inns, shrines and pagodas, and whenever you finish a quest.

## Controls

- **Arrow keys:** move (relative to the camera)
- **Shift or C:** sprint
- **X:** jump. In the air, hold it to glide or press it again to double jump (once unlocked)
- **Z:** katana combo, chop, mine. In the air: plunge attack
- **A:** talk, pick up, open, cook, pray
- **S:** throw a shuriken
- **Q / E:** rotate the camera
- **Tab or I:** bag and crafting. **M:** map. **J:** quest journal
- **1 to 0:** use a hotbar item (eat, equip, light, place)

Push into any wall to climb it, and press X while climbing to leap up.

## Made with TIC-80

KAGE runs on the TIC-80 fantasy console at 240×136 pixels and 60 fps. It ships without a single asset file: every sprite, texture, 3D building, sound effect and song is generated by code when the game boots, and so is the whole island. Under the hood is a small z-buffered 3D renderer: buildings, pagodas, torii gates and Kyo Castle are polygon models, and characters and trees are billboards.
